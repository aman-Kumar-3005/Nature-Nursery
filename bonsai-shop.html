How to Care for Your Bonsai at Home

Bonsai trees are long-lived companions. With basic attentiveness to watering, sunlight, and pruning, your tree will flourish and mature gracefully over the years.

The Touch Test for Watering Never water on a strict timetable. Water thoroughly when the top 1 cm of soil feels slightly dry to the touch until water drains freely from the pot.
Adequate Light is Vital Place indoor bonsai near a south or east-facing window receiving 4-6 hours of natural bright indirect light.
