Privacy Policy

Your privacy and communication security are of paramount importance to Nature Nursery, Indore.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an online enquiry form or initiate a chat via WhatsApp on naturenursery.in, we may collect your name, contact telephone number, email address (if provided), and the specific plants or landscaping services you have enquired about.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is strictly utilized to:

  • Respond to your plant availability queries, stock prices, and delivery quotes.
  • Coordinate landscaping site visits and project consultations.
  • Process interstate packing, dispatch logistics, and courier tracking details.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information with any third-party marketing companies.

3. Communication via WhatsApp & Phone

By clicking to enquire via WhatsApp or submitting a callback request on our website, you consent to our team communicating with you via WhatsApp or telephone solely regarding your plant or landscaping inquiry. You may opt-out or request cessation of communication at any time by notifying our desk.

4. Security of Information

We maintain administrative and operational safeguards to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
