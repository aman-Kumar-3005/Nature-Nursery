Terms & Conditions

Guidelines governing nursery grounds access, plant health expectations, delivery, and contractual agreements.

1. Living Plant Disclaimer

Plants, trees, and Bonsai are perishable living organisms. Their ongoing health and growth depend directly upon the post-purchase environment provided by the buyer (including sunlight, adequate watering, soil moisture, and pest management). While we guarantee that all specimens are dispatched or handed over in healthy, root-sound condition from our nursery, we cannot provide unconditional warranties on long-term survival once transplanted or situated.

2. Pricing & Catalog Variations

Prices displayed on naturenursery.in (including Bonsai price points) serve as current baseline references. Botanical specimens naturally vary in physical girth, height, and seasonal foliage maturity. Final invoice pricing will be confirmed with our sales team prior to dispatch or purchase.

3. Nursery Grounds & Visitor Guidelines

Visitors enter the Nature Nursery grounds at their own discretion. Given the sprawling, working horticultural environment with outdoor terrain, watering lines, and gravel paths, visitors are expected to exercise care, wear appropriate walking footwear, and supervise accompanying children.

4. Packaging & Interstate Transport

Long-distance transport is undertaken using specialized moisture-retaining wrapping and reinforced crating. In the rare event of transit damage, customers must notify our desk within 24 hours of delivery receipt accompanied by photographs of the unopened or unpacked crate.

5. Landscaping Contracting

All landscaping, lawn turf laying, and irrigation contracting work is executed under signed project estimates specifying milestones, soil preparation protocols, plant counts, and warranty clauses agreed upon in writing.
