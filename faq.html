Know Before You Go: Pro Visitor Tips

Grounds are extensive: We recommend wearing comfortable walking shoes as a full tour takes 45 to 90 minutes.
Best visiting hours: Morning hours (9:00 AM – 11:30 AM) and late afternoons are the coolest and most serene.
Plant Identification: Due to continuous new arrivals, ask our on-ground staff (like Veer) for tagging help.

Nature Nursery operates sprawling nursery grounds near Regional Park in Indore. Because our facility spans acres of covered greenhouses, open-air tree groves, and specialized bonsai pavilions, exploring involves a genuine nature walk. We strongly recommend comfortable footwear, breathable outdoor clothing, and sun protection during midday visits.

With thousands of botanical varieties, our dedicated on-ground attendants (such as our commended staff member Veer) are present throughout the aisles to personally guide you. You can also browse our digital Plant Varieties page beforehand and show your shortlisted photos directly to the attendant at the reception desk.
