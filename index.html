🧭 Know Before You Go: Helpful Tips for Exploring Our Grounds

Nature Nursery spans expansive acres of outdoor botanical trails, shaded greenhouse rows, and specimen displays. To ensure you have an enjoyable visit:

👟 Wear Comfortable Walking Shoes: Because our nursery grounds are vast and scenic, exploring our living collections involves a pleasant 45–90 min walk.
🏷️ Personalized Botanical Tagging: With thousands of species, our on-site team (including Veer) are readily available to assist with sunlight and soil matching.
🚚 Interstate & City Transport: We provide specialized custom-crated packing, delivering specimen trees and delicate bonsai safely across Madhya Pradesh and India.
