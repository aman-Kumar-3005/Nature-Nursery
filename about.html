🌿 About Nature Nursery, Indore ✨

Understanding the living needs of gardens, farmhouses, and urban spaces with Central India's most expansive plant sanctuary.

🌱 Our Heritage & Space

A Living Botanical Haven in the Heart of Indore 🪴

Nestled along Agra Bombay Road beside Regional Park, Nature Nursery was born from a singular mission: to provide garden lovers, architects, and institutions with an unmatched diversity of healthy flora under one roof.

We truly understand "the needs of gardens and spaces". Whether you are seeking a pocket-sized desk succulent, an artistically sculpted 20-year-old Bonsai, or full-grown avenue canopy trees for a 5-acre farmhouse estate, our sprawling grounds allow you to hand-pick plants directly from root to leaf.

With ⭐️ 4.7★ rating across 913+ verified Google reviews, we have earned our standing as Central India's trusted green landmark, serving both wholesale landscape contractors and retail plant lovers with equal dedication.

On-Ground Guidance

Designed for an Unforgettable Botanical Walk

Because our nursery spans acres of outdoor space, we want every visitor to be prepared for an effortless, enjoyable exploration.

Wear Comfortable Walking Shoes

Our grounds are expansive. Walking through our shaded groves, open-air tree aisles, and bonsai galleries is a genuine nature walk—so comfortable footwear is highly recommended!

Staff Tagging & Expert Help

With thousands of varieties, finding a specific cultivar is made easy by our knowledgeable on-ground attendants (like our commended staff member Veer) who will guide you to the exact section.

Wholesale Scale & Transparent Value

We operate at scale. From individual collector specimens with premium aesthetic cultivation to bulk truckloads of boundary hedging, we cater to both homeowners and commercial developers.

Our Philosophy

Rooted in Passion, Nurtured for Longevity

Plants are not simply decorative commodities; they are living ecosystems that elevate air quality, calm the spirit, and enhance real estate aesthetics. At Nature Nursery, we focus on:

  • Acclimatized Root Systems: Plants conditioned for central India's temperatures and soil balances so they transplant smoothly into your garden.
  • Ethical Cultivation: Enriched organic potting mixes, balanced micro-nutrients, and gentle pest management.
  • After-Purchase Guidance: Educational plant care support through our recurring "Green Guide" videos and direct consultation.
